A. Signed mixed numbers

  1. \(-\dfrac{9}{2}+\dfrac{10}{3}=-\dfrac{27}{6}+\dfrac{20}{6}=-\dfrac{7}{6}=-1\dfrac{1}{6}\)
  2. \(-\dfrac{13}{5}+\dfrac{3}{2}=-\dfrac{26}{10}+\dfrac{15}{10}=-\dfrac{11}{10}=-1\dfrac{1}{10}\)
  3. \(-\dfrac{7}{6}+\dfrac{11}{4}=-\dfrac{14}{12}+\dfrac{33}{12}=\dfrac{19}{12}=1\dfrac{7}{12}\)
  4. \(-\dfrac{11}{3}+\dfrac{9}{4}=-\dfrac{44}{12}+\dfrac{27}{12}=-\dfrac{17}{12}=-1\dfrac{5}{12}\)
  5. \(-\dfrac{17}{8}+\dfrac{7}{2}=-\dfrac{17}{8}+\dfrac{28}{8}=\dfrac{11}{8}=1\dfrac{3}{8}\)
  6. \(-\dfrac{23}{4}+\dfrac{22}{5}=-\dfrac{115}{20}+\dfrac{88}{20}=-\dfrac{27}{20}=-1\dfrac{7}{20}\)

C. Exponents

  1. \(5^{2+3+0}=5^5=3125\)
  2. \(8^{7-4}=8^3=512\)
  3. \(3^{6+0-2}=3^4=81\)
  4. \(10^{0+4-1}=10^3=1000\)
  5. \(6^{5-2-0}=6^3=216\)
  6. \(9^m \cdot 9^0 = 9^{m+0}=9^m\), so multiplying by \(9^0\) changes nothing. The only such number is 1.
