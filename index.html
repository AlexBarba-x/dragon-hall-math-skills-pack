A kitchen-table hall

Four math skills, taught like a real lesson.

This pack is for one learner and one grown-up. Each lesson follows Dragon Hall Commons beats: say the move, do the move, listen for the right idea. Practice is original. The adult keeps the answer key.

Print practice packet Open a lesson

How a sitting works

1. Grown-up reads the gold Say lines aloud.

2. Learner does the green Do step on paper.

3. Grown-up listens for the peach line, then hides the key.

4. One short check on screen. Paper practice last.

Integer exponent laws

Count factors to multiply and divide powers of the same base. A zero exponent is 1, because the product law says so.

Base pay and percent of sales

Name the base. Earnings = base pay + rate × sales. Work both ways: sales to pay, and pay back to sales.

For the grown-up

You are the teacher in the room. Confirm every grade. Do not leave the adult reveal open on the table. If a check is shaky, stop and reteach the beat that slipped. Do not lower the bar.

Print the combined packet with the key on its own page. Tear that page off before the learner sits down.
